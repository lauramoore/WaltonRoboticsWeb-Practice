Who we are

Team 2974 operates like a professional engineering organization built by students. From our design reviews to our outreach programs, we hold ourselves to executive-level standards of communication, documentation, and accountability — because that's the standard the FIRST community deserves, and the standard that prepares our members for what comes after high school.

Every season, our subteams — mechanical, electrical, programming, business, and media — work as one organization toward a single robot and a single mission: build excellent engineers and excellent people.

Walt & WaterGate

Walt is our mascot and WaterGate is our current competition robot — the latest step in a build tradition that has carried Team 2974 to events across the country and around the world.

Impact & community outreach

Walton Robotics runs three ongoing programs that bring engineering to students long before they reach an FRC field.

GirlsFIRST

Empowering women in STEM through dedicated community events built to grow the next generation of female engineers.

FIRST LEGO League & FTC

Supporting Walton's junior robotics programs and hosting local FLL and FTC tournaments throughout the year.

Destination Engineering & Summer Camps

Hands-on youth camps and year-round practice field management that turn curiosity into real engineering skill.
